# bento-deck-studio

Turn a PowerPoint theme into editable bento slides, then share them with a launch page or a companion site.

## Keep your brand. Skip the rebuild.
<!-- layout: statement -->

## How it works
1. **Extract** the theme: colors, fonts, slide size, masters, and layouts.
2. **Author** the story in Markdown, one idea per slide.
3. **Build** native bento slides on the source's own layouts.
4. **Share** a launch page or a full companion site.

## Built to match the source
- **Proportions:** every placeholder keeps its source position and scale.
- **Colors:** theme colors resolve through each master's color map.
- **Typography:** licensed fonts are embedded and calibrated to PowerPoint metrics.

## Share it your way
- A one-page launch site by default.
- An optional full site with **Slides ↗** first in the top menu.
- A favicon from your image, or a monogram in your theme colors.
- Static output for GitHub Pages or any web host.
